### SPIRE Fellows Program
_Fall 2024 – Spring 2026 – Duke University_

- Tutored undergraduate fellows in SPIRE, a Duke STEM fellowship that broadens participation in the sciences and funds each fellow for one-on-one tutoring with a graduate student.
- Met individually with students on their own coursework, on a schedule set by what each student needed rather than a fixed curriculum.
